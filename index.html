Quick answer

What is Bridgelands?

Bridgelands is one of Vaughan's last major greenfield master-planned communities, at Weston Road & Teston Road in North Vaughan, within the Block 41 planning area bounded by Kirby Road, Weston Road, Teston Road and Pine Valley Drive. The 428-hectare planning area is planned with townhomes, detached homes, mid-rise and mixed-use residential, plus retail, schools, parks, trails, a community centre and library. Pricing is on request, with a public launch on November 10, 2026.

About the community

Master-Planned Bridgelands

Bridgelands is one of Vaughan's last major greenfield New Community Areas — approximately 428 hectares in North Vaughan. Ontario Regulation 644/20 permits a mix of residential, commercial, institutional and recreational uses. Multiple builders are releasing townhomes and detached homes starting November 10, 2026.

  • Approved Secondary Plan. OPA 50 fully approved by the OLT in August 2021.
  • Fully serviced infrastructure. Teston Rd widened to 4 lanes with sewers, watermain, cycling paths and lighting.
  • Multi-builder community. Arista, Deco and Opus Homes — registrants hear about releases first.

Planned Land Uses

Per approved OPA 50 and O. Reg. 644/20. The Block 41 Secondary Plan permits a diverse mix of housing and community uses.

  • Low-rise residential (towns & detached)
  • Low-rise mixed-use zones
  • Mid-rise residential
  • Mid-rise mixed-use community core
  • Planned schools, parks & trails
  • Planned community centre & library
